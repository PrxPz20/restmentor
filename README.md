# RestMentor

**AI-powered restaurant order management for the modern waiter.**

RestMentor is a SaaS platform that helps restaurant waitstaff take orders faster and smarter. As items are added to an order, an AI engine analyzes the table — who's there, what they've ordered, and what would genuinely complement their meal — then suggests the next item with clear reasoning the waiter can use as a natural talking point with the customer.

The platform earns revenue only when AI suggestions convert to real sales. No monthly fees for restaurants. No risk. Just performance.

---

## What It Does

**For waiters**
Open a table, set the guest composition (males, females, kids), and start taking orders by gender group. The AI works in the background — by the time the first items are in, suggestions are ready. Each suggestion comes with 2–3 bullet points explaining exactly why it fits the table, giving the waiter a confident, natural way to pitch it.

**For kitchens and bars**
Orders appear on dedicated display screens the moment the waiter taps Send. Food goes to the kitchen display, drinks go to the bar display — automatically routed based on the menu setup.

**For restaurant managers**
A full admin panel to manage the menu, staff accounts, and tables. An AI performance dashboard shows which suggestions converted, which items are most often recommended, and how much revenue the AI has driven.

**For the platform**
A super admin panel to onboard restaurants, set commission rates, and track platform-wide revenue across all restaurants.

---

## How the AI Works

When a table opens, the AI is briefed with the full restaurant menu and the guest composition. Each time a waiter adds an item, the AI responds with two suggestions tailored to that specific table — considering what's already been ordered, who the guests are, and what naturally fits next. Suggestions that the waiter accepts are tracked, and when the table pays, the platform earns a commission on those AI-driven items only.

## The Five Portals

| Portal | Who Uses It |
|---|---|
| **Waiter App** | Waitstaff on tablets and smartphones |
| **Kitchen Display** | Kitchen team — shows food orders in real time |
| **Bar Display** | Bar team — shows drink orders in real time |
| **Restaurant Admin** | Restaurant owners and managers |
| **Platform Super Admin** | RestMentor team |

---

## Key Principles

- **Performance-based** — restaurants only pay when AI suggestions generate real revenue
- **Real-time** — orders appear on displays the moment they're sent, no refresh needed
- **Multi-tenant** — every restaurant is completely isolated with its own database
- **Fast** — built to work smoothly on a busy restaurant floor, on any tablet or phone
